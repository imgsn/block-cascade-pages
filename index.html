BLOCK CASCADE

Privacy Policy

Summary

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Block Cascade is a falling-block puzzle game for Android. You can play without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, the game saves your best score online and can show a game name and avatar that you choose, with your best score, on a public leaderboard. If you never sign in, nothing leaves your device.

Information we collect

If you do not sign in: nothing. Your best score, sound and theme settings are stored only on your device.

If you sign in with Google:

  • A unique account ID created by Firebase Authentication.
  • A game name and one of the built-in avatars, both chosen by you. Please use a nickname, not your real name.
  • Your best score and the time it was last updated.

Firebase Authentication also keeps the email address, name and profile picture link that Google provides with the sign-in. The game does not read, store or show them: it never uses your Google name or picture.

How we use it

  • To keep your best score when you reinstall the app or change phones.
  • To show the leaderboard: your game name, avatar and best score are visible to other players of the game.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone other than the service providers below.

Service providers

Sign-in and storage are provided by Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google Sign-In), which processes the data on our behalf under Google's privacy policy.

Data on your device

The best score, sound setting and theme are saved in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only for sign-in, saving your best score and loading the leaderboard. The game works offline without them.
  • Vibration is used for game events. Sound effects are bundled and play locally. The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Ads and analytics

Block Cascade contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

You can delete your online data at any time, inside the app: tap the account icon, then Delete my online data and account. This removes your leaderboard entry (game name, avatar and score) and your sign-in account. See the account deletion page for details. The best score saved on your phone stays until you uninstall the app.

Children

Sign-in and the leaderboard are intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Playing without signing in collects nothing from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/imgsn/block-cascade-pages/issues. Please do not post personal information there.
