BLOCK CASCADE

Privacy Policy

Summary

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Block Cascade is a falling-block puzzle game for Android. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Block Cascade does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers or advertising ID.

Data stored on your device

The game saves one piece of data: your best score. It is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device so it can be shown the next time you play. It is never sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions, sensors and network

  • The app does not request the internet permission and makes no network connections.
  • Sound effects are bundled with the app and play locally. The app does not use the microphone or camera.
  • Vibration (haptic feedback) is used for game events such as clearing a line. No data is collected from it.

Ads, analytics and third parties

Block Cascade contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services that receive your data.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. It is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle personal data, such as online accounts or leaderboards, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/imgsn/block-cascade-pages/issues.
