Account deletion

Delete your Block Cascade account

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Block Cascade accounts are optional and only exist if you chose to sign in with Google. You can delete yours, and the data tied to it, at any time.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Block Cascade and tap the account icon at the top.
  2. Tap Delete my online data and account.
  3. Confirm. If asked, sign in with Google again so we can confirm it is you.

What is deleted

  • Your leaderboard entry: game name, avatar, best score and update time.
  • Your sign-in account, including the email and profile link Firebase Authentication stored for it.

The best score saved on your phone is not an online account. It stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

If you can no longer use the app

Reinstall Block Cascade, sign in with the same Google account, and use the steps above. If that is not possible, open an issue at github.com/imgsn/block-cascade-pages/issues titled "Delete my data". Do not include personal information; we will reply there with how to confirm your identity.
